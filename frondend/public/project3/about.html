Our Story

Where Tradition Meets Innovation: A Culinary Journey of Passion and Excellence

Our Mission

The Progress Restaurant was founded with a bold vision: bring together the vibrant, aromatic flavors of Japanese cuisine and the timeless, comforting classics of Italian cooking in one welcoming, warm space.

We believe that food is more than just sustenance—it's about creating memorable experiences, bringing communities together, and celebrating the art of cooking with passion and authenticity.

📍
👨‍🍳

Our Philosophy

Our chefs carefully select fresh, premium ingredients every single day from trusted local suppliers and prepare each dish with meticulous attention to detail. Whether you're craving our signature peri-peri chicken, authentic pasta carbonara, or a comforting biryani, we want every plate to feel like it was made just for you.

Quality, freshness, and excellence are not just standards—they're our commitment to every customer who walks through our doors.
