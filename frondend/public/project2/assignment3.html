The AirPods 4 represent Apple’s newest generation of wireless earbuds, arriving in two versions: a standard model and a model with Active Noise Cancellation (ANC). Both versions introduce meaningful upgrades in sound quality, comfort, and smart features, making them a strong step forward from previous generations.

  • Open-ear design
  • Comfortable long-term wear
  • Adaptive EQ
  • Noise Control
  • Call clarity
  • Total 30 hours with the charging case
